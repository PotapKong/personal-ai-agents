Privacy policy

This policy describes the Google data access used by Hughie and Lapulya, personal AI assistants for their owner.

Access and use

The owner grants access to Google data through OAuth. The assistants use that data exclusively to fulfill the owner's requests.

Sharing

Google data is not sold or shared with advertising networks.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the repository's GitHub Issues.
